# Previous academic-year attendance — founder checklist

Use https://kids-corner-erp-frontend.vercel.app/login and the private historical-year QA accounts. Test **Dashboard → Official attendance** and **Attendance → Official attendance**. Founder acceptance is independent of engineering QA; leave these boxes unchecked until you run them. API and access/setup cases YR-06–08 need developer or School Admin assistance.

The approved formula remains Present/Late = 1, Half Day = 0.5, Leave/Absent = 0. School-closed days are excluded. Missing or inconsistent records withhold a percentage. Previous-year reports use that year's enrollment, historical section placement and school calendar; later approved corrections can update a report.

- [ ] YR-01: Student starts on Current session. In the prepared QA school the current rate is **100%**. Select **2025–26**; monthly March 2026 and academic-session rates become **58.33%**, with 6 working days, 1 closed day, 3.5 earned days and no unmarked workday. The cutoff is **31 March 2026**, not today.
- [ ] YR-02: Hard refresh after explicitly choosing the previous year. That pupil's selected year remains selected and fresh verified figures reload. Another account or child does not inherit this preference. If browser session storage is unavailable, the page still works with its Current session default.
- [ ] YR-03: Within 2025–26 select a month before the pupil's 25 March admission. The monthly panel has no eligible enrollment days or percentage; the full-session panel remains 58.33%. Switch back to Current session: the reporting month resets to the current session's valid default and the rate becomes 100%.
- [ ] YR-04: Parent sees exactly the two linked QA children. Complete child's previous year is 58.33%; missing sibling's previous year is **Awaiting attendance**, with one unmarked day and no percentage. Both children's current year is 100%. Rapid child/year switches must not leave another child's label or rate visible.
- [ ] YR-05: Open the Attendance page. Its official report has the same year/month controls. The separate raw history is explicitly across all academic years; it retains March 27 Half Day and March 28 Present even though March 28 is a school holiday. Earlier section A records continue to count after the historical move to section B and promotion into the new class.
- [ ] YR-06: Future/staged years and an unenrolled old year are absent from the selectable report list. Direct API requests for foreign pupils, foreign-school years or an unowned archived year are denied. Student cannot view the sibling; Parent cannot view an unlinked child. Admin/Teacher role labels do not grant family report access.
- [ ] YR-07: Revoke only a disposable QA child link through the governed Admin flow. That Parent loses both current and previous-year reports for that child; the independent sibling link still works. A legitimate re-invite/verified acceptance restores the intended child's access without changing attendance. Do this only with isolated QA accounts and preserve the private invite token.
- [ ] YR-08: A school with missing, ambiguous or unsupported current-year setup shows that issue without silently choosing an old year. An available completed year can still be selected. Missing calendar, contradictory placement or unverified withdrawal evidence produces an explicit unavailable/review state and no invented 0% or 100%.
- [ ] YR-09: Test phone width, keyboard navigation, year/month labels, skeletons, retry and dark/light appearance. During a slow year change the old percentage and year label disappear. Access errors clear old report/catalog data. Refresh after a year activation must not mix metadata from two sessions.
- [ ] YR-10: Log out all test accounts. Retained logged-out sessions cannot read the report or its year list. Compare original attendance, fees, links and inbox/read state; viewing reports must not write those records or send notifications.

The prepared admission, historical placement, marks and calendar are explicit synthetic reporting fixtures. This checklist does not claim that native APIs accepted backdated roster/calendar writes. School approval of actual enrollment/calendar records and manual founder acceptance remain open. The native app's current report API stays compatible; this release adds the web year selector.
