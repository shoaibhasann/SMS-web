## Flow and boundaries

Admin/Principal → Timetable → school/session/class/view section → Add period → eligible subject/teacher assignment → weekday, period number, section, start/end and optional room → Save. **Saving shares the period immediately.** Teacher sees its own current assigned periods; Student and currently linked Guardian see the child's current active class schedule. These views are read-only.

Times are school wall-clock times in IST, in whole minutes, within one day. Overlaps use `[start,end)`: 08:00–09:00 next to 09:00–10:00 is allowed. Conflict checks apply within the school, academic session and weekday. A NULL period section means **Whole class (all sections)**. Office **All sections** is a viewing filter; it shows all section rows plus whole-class periods. Selecting a specific viewing section includes that section and whole-class rows. No school working-day, break-duration or room-booking policy is inferred.

## Founder cases

- [ ] **TT-01 · Create and refresh.** As Admin/Principal, choose the intended school, session and class. Add an eligible subject/teacher period with room, save once and reload the browser. Expect the same weekday, period, section, teacher, subject, start/end and room. No browser demo rows should appear. A saved change becomes visible to authorized readers immediately.
- [ ] **TT-02 · Teacher timing conflict.** Schedule one teacher in Class A at 08:00–09:00 on Monday. Try another class/section for that teacher at 08:30–09:30, an identical interval, a contained interval and an interval enclosing the first. All must reject without a new record; dates/times and draft remain editable. Try 09:00–10:00: it may save if its class is free. Another weekday/session is a separate weekly scope.
- [ ] **TT-03 · Class/section conflict and period identity.** With different teachers, try overlapping periods for the same class/section. Reject. Try the same class/section/day/period number at a non-overlapping time: reject the duplicate period identity. Different sections may run in parallel with different teachers. Whole-class periods must conflict with every section's overlapping timing or duplicate period number, in both insertion orders.
- [ ] **TT-04 · Mapping and validation.** Choose only active eligible teacher/subject assignments. A section-specific assignment cannot schedule a whole-class or other-section period. A classwide assignment may schedule a valid concrete section. Wrong-school/year/class/subject/teacher/section combinations, nonpositive periods, reversed/equal/overnight times, nonzero seconds and room labels over 120 characters must fail. The UI uses minute precision. Inactive teacher/profile/role or removed assignment cannot be reused through a direct request.
- [ ] **TT-05 · Edit, conflict and recovery.** Open a saved period in two authorized office sessions. Save a change in one; try the old draft in the other. Expect a stale-version conflict, no overwrite and preserved draft. List Refresh must not silently replace the draft's original version. Cancel/reopen the draft to review current data before saving. A conflicting move must leave the original period intact.
- [ ] **TT-06 · Retry and unknown outcome.** Double-click Save and simulate connection loss after a save/remove. Retrying the unchanged action must create one canonical outcome. Keep its retry reference and baseline across an unconfirmed response. Reusing the reference for changed data or another operation rejects. Refresh saved records before deciding an unconfirmed request failed. Errors must not look like a successfully empty timetable.
- [ ] **TT-07 · Remove and preserve evidence.** Remove a saved period through its confirmation. Reload office, teacher and family views: it is absent. Its slot is free for a new period. An old editor/removal version must fail; the original period stays as an immutable tombstone and private before/after receipt. Attendance, fees, receipts and teaching assignments are unchanged; timetable operations must not grant teaching authority.
- [ ] **TT-08 · Teacher and family readers.** Teacher reloads and sees only its own current valid assigned teaching periods. Student sees only its current active class/section and whole-class periods. Guardian switches siblings and reloads without mixing schedules. Wrong/unlinked child and wrong-school IDs deny. Staged/future/cancelled/withdrawn enrollments do not expose a future/current schedule. An account with no current active enrollment receives an explicit empty context.
- [ ] **TT-09 · Revocation and mixed roles.** Remove a teacher role/assignment and refresh: its old scheduled periods no longer appear in teacher/family views until the school repairs the mapping. Revoke one guardian-child link: subsequent reads deny that child while another linked sibling remains available. Disabled/unverified/banned/deleted accounts deny. Finance/Reception cannot manage timetables. Mixed Teacher/Guardian uses `?view=guardian` for child schedules; the normal Teacher route remains its own schedule. Attendance-only substitute coverage does not create timetable authority.
- [ ] **TT-10 · Legacy repair.** If an older period has missing times/assignment, office sees Needs attention; teacher/family never see it as a valid schedule. Repair it with an eligible mapping and valid times, or remove it. An incomplete older row blocks conflicting teacher/class-day booking until resolved. Do not invent times or discard old rows to make a migration pass. Existing duplicate/overlapping timed data can stop constraint installation atomically and requires reviewed correction.
- [ ] **TT-11 · Phone, keyboard and themes.** At 390 px dark and 1440 px light, controls, day cards, status/error messages and confirmations remain readable with no page overflow. Child/school selectors retain readable width. Tab through fields: labels and focus ring remain stable after changing values. Save/remove locks conflicting context changes; an unsaved draft requires explicit discard. Delayed responses from an old child/class/school/account cannot replace current data.
- [ ] **TT-12 · Concurrent database acceptance.** Run two real PostgreSQL connections saving conflicting periods at once, including classwide-versus-section. Exactly one may commit. Repeat edit/remove races. Native GiST exclusion constraints exist for teacher overlap, class/section overlap and duplicate period identity; availability queries alone are insufficient. Local serial tests and the controlled browser bridge do not tick this hosted concurrency case.
