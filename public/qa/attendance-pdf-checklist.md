# Official attendance PDF - founder checklist

Use https://sms-web-shoaibhasann.vercel.app/login and the private historical-year Student/Guardian QA accounts. Check **Dashboard > Official attendance** and **Attendance > Official attendance**. Leave boxes unchecked until you run them. PDF-07 needs developer or School Admin assistance. If you change scope or refocus the app during preparation, let the report finish refreshing and click Download PDF again.

- [ ] PDF-01: Student starts on Current session. Download PDF and open the actual file: correct school, student/admission number, current academic year, selected reporting month, generated IST time and cutoff. The prepared current-year rate is **100%**.
- [ ] PDF-02: Select **2025-26**, March 2026, then Download PDF. Monthly and academic-session sections both show **58.33%**, 6 working days, 1 closed day excluded, 3.5 earned days and 0 unmarked days. Cutoff is **31 March 2026**. Present/Late = 1, Half Day = 0.5, Leave/Absent = 0.
- [ ] PDF-03: In 2025-26 select February, before the QA pupil's 25 March admission. PDF identifies **February 2026**, with no eligible monthly rate; the full academic-session rate remains 58.33%. No made-up 0% should appear. Switch Current and check the next PDF uses its valid current month and year.
- [ ] PDF-04: Guardian sees only linked children. Complete child's old PDF is 58.33%; missing sibling's old PDF explicitly says attendance is awaited, with one unmarked working day and no percentage. Check the student identity changes correctly in each file. Missing records are not assumed Absent.
- [ ] PDF-05: Hard refresh after choosing a previous year, then export. The restored year and visible month match the PDF. Repeat from the Attendance page. Downloaded files remain readable without the app and include both monthly and academic-session summaries.
- [ ] PDF-06: Use keyboard/phone width and Light/Dark appearance. Download button has a useful busy state and prevents repeated clicks. Rapid child/year/month switches or logout during export must cancel the old action. A failed network/font/access check shows retry guidance without downloading a stale report.
- [ ] PDF-07: With isolated QA access, attempt an unlinked pupil, foreign/unenrolled/future year or wrong role. No PDF is downloaded. After a school-authorized revoke, that Guardian cannot export the revoked child's report; an independently linked child remains available. Only a legitimate invite acceptance should restore access.
- [ ] PDF-08: Open PDFs in your normal reader and print preview. Confirm A4 layout, readable headings/table/footer, page numbers and no clipped long names. Hindi/Latin names must render correctly; unsupported characters should produce a clear export error, never silently corrupt a name.
- [ ] PDF-09: Log out test accounts. Original attendance, fees, child links, inbox/read status and calendar records remain unchanged. Export does not send email/SMS/push or create a hosted/public file link.

Prepared reporting records are synthetic QA fixtures. The PDF reflects the verified records when downloaded and may change after legitimate corrections. It is not digitally signed. Once downloaded, that local file cannot be remotely revoked. Native-app export and daily-row/class/school PDFs are separate work.
