# Gate operations - founder checklist

Use https://kids-corner-erp-frontend.vercel.app/login with a private QA School Admin/Principal account and its authenticator. Open **Attendance > Gate operations**. These boxes are founder acceptance; leave them unchecked until you test them. Use disposable school records for writes.

- [ ] GO-01: Open Gate operations. Check the verified school, active device, retained device history, student identifier mappings and event receipts. Refresh: all confirmed records remain. The daily register remains available separately.
- [ ] GO-02: As an actual School Admin/Principal with native MFA, configure a disposable device with its name, IST late cutoff and explicitly selected fallback weekdays. One school permits one active gateway. Its configured school calendar overrides fallback weekdays. A second active gateway must be rejected.
- [ ] GO-03: Reveal the newly confirmed device credential only when needed and copy it manually into the authorized connector. It must not appear in URLs, history lists, exports, localStorage or sessionStorage. Closing/reloading clears the credential; it cannot be recovered from saved setup. If lost, revoke and replace the gateway. Never share a school staff password or Supabase server key with a device.
- [ ] GO-04: Search by at least two characters of an admission number or name, select the exact pupil and map its device identifier. Matching names alone must not silently select a pupil. Reject duplicate pupil/subject mappings. Revoke a mapping with a reason; keep its history and use a new identifier for a replacement card. A retired identifier must not be reassigned.
- [ ] GO-05: Check accepted and held IN/OUT receipts, including device time, receipt time and reason. The list is paginated; the displayed page is not a schoolwide held-count claim. Receipt attendance values describe ingestion time, not a live replacement for the canonical register.
- [ ] GO-06: A held unknown-identifier, future-clock, historical or conflicting scan remains held after a mapping is added or revoked. This screen must not silently replay/approve it or overwrite attendance. Use the established attendance review/correction workflow for verified changes. OUT must not erase daily presence.
- [ ] GO-07: Revoke a disposable gateway with a reason. Mapping history and event receipts remain. The connector's old credential, including retries of old events, must be rejected. A replacement gateway gets a new credential and its own pupil mappings.
- [ ] GO-08: During an uncertain save, retry the exact frozen action instead of generating another credential/mapping. Double clicks must not create duplicates. An exact retry returning a retired record must not claim it was reactivated or display its credential as usable.
- [ ] GO-09: Change account/school, refocus the window, refresh and sign out. Old data and sensitive credential panels must clear. Teacher, Finance, Parent, Student, no-role, foreign-school, AAL1 and logged-out sessions must be denied direct setup/search/write APIs; hiding buttons alone is insufficient.
- [ ] GO-10: Check 390px width, keyboard controls, readable labels, skeletons, Light/Dark and error recovery. Verify attendance, fee/history, guardian links, inbox/read preferences and calendar remain unchanged by gate management. Log out test accounts.

The prepared automated QA uses synthetic held-only scans and fresh revocable device/mapping rows. It does not certify a physical device, a vendor adapter, real arrival/exit accuracy or delivery to a phone. A missing scan stays Unmarked; this screen does not generate absence or activate paid messaging/push.
