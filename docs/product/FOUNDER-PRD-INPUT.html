Where the product stands

Attendance

In the local app connected to the hosted demo database, an assigned class teacher and a school admin saved, corrected and reloaded marks. A student saw their own history. A parent switched between linked children and could not see an unrelated pupil’s marks.

Assignments, fees and academics

Admins and principals have an assignment screen and teachers can view their own assignments. Fee and receipt screens, classwork, homework and marks have database connections. Fee reconciliation and the full academic publishing and results workflows still need work and acceptance tests.

People and academic setup include browser demo screens. QA accounts and guardian links were prepared for testing. A school still needs a way to bring in its roster and invite guardians. Preview sign-off has its own checklist.

Proposal for your decision

One school, one roster, one complete attendance workflow

Target a Delhi NCR private school with 300–1,500 students. A pilot school is still to be confirmed. Set up a small set of sections and approved staff, then run daily attendance and guardian history through real school days. Add guardian invitations and the minimum roster setup needed for that pilot.

  • Suggested pilot test: 10 school days, at least 90% of section registers submitted by the school’s cutoff, and zero wrong-child or wrong-school access in permission tests.
  • Suggested deferrals: fee posting and online payments, term report cards, outbound messaging, transport, library, payroll and inventory. Agree any exception before development.
Proposed sequence

What we would build in each phase

  1. Stabilise the existing app. Verify attendance and role access in Preview. Close the permission and data integrity issues that block the selected workflow.
  2. Set up the first school. Store real classes, sections, enrolments and staff assignments. Import the pilot roster and verify the guardian invitation and access removal flow.
  3. Complete one paid office workflow. If the school prioritises fees, start with one fee cycle covering opening dues, partial payments, receipts, approved reversals and day closing.
  4. Finish daily school workflows. Prioritise guardian attendance and receipt history, notices and homework. Scope approved marks, report cards and year rollover separately.
  5. Expand when schools ask and will pay. Evaluate admissions, transport, library, payroll, inventory and integrations against repeated demand and support cost.
