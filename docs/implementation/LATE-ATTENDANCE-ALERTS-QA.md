# School-controlled Guardian Late attendance alerts

7 November 2026. Release verification is in progress; founder acceptance is separate.

## Delivered scope

School Settings has a dedicated Late attendance policy for actual same-school School Admin or Principal sessions with native verified MFA. No policy row means OFF. A change requires the saved version, a new choice and a 5–300 character reason; accepted decisions are immutable. The UI retains an unknown write's exact request for reconciliation, blocks school changes while a draft/save is pending, and reloads current authority after acknowledgements, including old exact-replay acknowledgements.

A future committed current-IST-day canonical transition to Late creates one inbox event per currently eligible linked Guardian only when the school policy is ON. This covers the existing daily register, including eligible device-driven canonical marks. Enabling the policy does not backfill old marks, and re-saving an already Late row does not fabricate events. Duplicate creation is constrained by attendance/recipient identity. A short shared/exclusive school policy fence orders creation against policy changes; authorization and the current IST day are checked again after relevant waits.

The v3 family inbox exposes Late as an attendance link, with server-owned unread totals, All/Unread filters, scoped single read and atomic Mark all. Read state belongs to the recipient. Current child linkage, account recovery identity and the still-Late canonical source are rechecked. Correction away from Late hides the event; returning to Late preserves its ID/read state for a surviving link. Removing a link removes that link's alert; re-linking alone cannot resurrect it. School OFF stops new creation while retained eligible Late records remain visible.

## Compatibility and limits

Existing v1/v2 APIs, native app source and prior attendance/gate/notification routines are not replaced. The web adopts additive v3 APIs. Student v3 has no Late events and cannot mutate or deep-link them. Existing absence/result/leave/gate behavior remains independently authorized. Arrival/exit preferences do not control Late or absence.

No Late native push jobs, phone delivery, email, SMS, mobile binary update, real-device activation or paid service is introduced. The separate app continues v2 until its parser/inbox is deliberately upgraded. Local deterministic coverage does not certify physical hardware, native phone delivery, school acceptance or managed concurrent load.
