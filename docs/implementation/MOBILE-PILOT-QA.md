### Earlier Storage CDN finding — legacy closure still open

At the earlier Learning checkpoint, fresh nonce/no-store reads denied revoked or unassigned identities, but a previously authorized raw URL still returned a CDN HIT after logout. The later proxy/origin hardening is implemented as recorded above; it does not by itself prove that older cached/signed capabilities are gone. Keep strict legacy-revocation claims gated on the owner’s independently verified key/cache closure. Downloaded copies cannot be recalled. [Official provider explanation](https://supabase.com/kb/troubleshooting/storage-private-bucket-files-still-accessible-after-remove-access-or-logout).

Persistent network/cleanup failures can leave unreferenced private uploads, requiring a retention sweep before wider distribution. See [remaining web/product work](WEB-REMAINING.md); this phase does not certify the complete commercial ERP.
